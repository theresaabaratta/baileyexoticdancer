What this page is, who runs it, and why it exists.

What this site is

Buffalo Male Entertainment Guide is a fan-style, independently run directory covering male exotic entertainment in the Buffalo, NY area. Think of it as a local guide built by enthusiasts of the scene — booking tips, event ideas, and a running list of independent entertainers worth knowing about.

What this site isn't

This is not an official site for any performer, agency, club, or venue. We aren't a booking agency ourselves, we don't take deposits, and we aren't authorized to speak on behalf of anyone listed here. Every entertainer that appears on this site runs their own independent business.

In short: we're fans of the local entertainment scene who built a directory to make it easier to find independent performers and know what to expect before booking. Nothing on this site should be read as an endorsement, partnership, or official statement from any listed entertainer.

Why we built it

Buffalo has a real, active independent entertainment scene, but it's scattered across social media, word of mouth, and one-off websites. This guide pulls that together in one place — practical booking advice up front, and an easy way to browse independent options at the bottom.

How the directory works

Entertainers on this site are listed independently and don't pay for placement. If you're an independent performer in the Buffalo area and want to be added, updated, or removed from the directory, reach out and we'll take care of it.

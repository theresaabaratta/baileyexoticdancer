Booking tips

A few things worth knowing before you hand over a deposit.

01

Confirm who you're actually booking

Ask directly whether you're booking a specific, named performer or whichever entertainer an agency assigns that night. Get the answer in writing before you pay anything.

02

Ask about deposits and cancellation terms

Independent entertainers and agencies vary widely here. Know what's refundable, what happens if the date changes, and what payment methods are accepted before booking.

03

Talk through the venue in advance

Private home, hotel suite, or rented venue each come with different expectations. Mention parking, noise, elevator access, and how many guests will be there.

04

Set expectations on show length and format

Performance length, music preferences, and how audience-interactive the show should be are all worth agreeing on ahead of time rather than figuring out on the night.

05

Get everything from the performer directly when possible

When booking an independent entertainer, confirm details (arrival time, contact number for the night of, address) directly with them rather than relying on a third party.

Retirement send-offs
